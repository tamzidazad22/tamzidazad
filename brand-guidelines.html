Midnight Founder

A premium, modern palette for a product leader, founder, and educator. Serious enough for payments and SaaS, elegant enough for teaching, ventures, and a personal archive.

Usage

How the palette should behave

Dominant feeling

Use Pearl and Ink as the foundation. Let the site feel calm, spacious, and editorial. Midnight should appear in strong moments, not everywhere.

Accent rule

Glacier should be restrained: labels, small dividers, subtle buttons, timeline markers, or a single highlight in a section.

Avoid

Avoid bright blue, heavy gradients, oversized cards, and too many accent surfaces. The site should feel composed, not decorative.

Typography Direction

Editorial, premium, readable

Wordmark direction

Tamzid Azad

Elegant serif/calligraphic wordmark. Refined, not ornamental. This can later become a custom logo treatment.

Sample page language

Product builder. Venture operator. Educator.

The body type should stay modern and clear. Pair a refined serif for headlines with a clean sans-serif for readable long-form career stories.
