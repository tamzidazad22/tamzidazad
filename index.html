Now · Founder & CEO

Building Doodlepad.

I am the Founder & CEO of Doodlepad, where we are building an operating system for homeschooling families and microschool operators. Doodlepad connects curriculum planning, curated learning resources, student practice, and progress tracking to create personalized, adaptive learning journeys.

Grounded in cognitive science, Doodlepad’s learning engine incorporates retrieval practice, spaced repetition, interleaving, adaptive difficulty, recognition-to-production progression, and informative feedback.

Doodlepad has launched an initial product with early adopters, been accepted into the YSpace Incubator, received $25,000 in Google for Startups credits, become a 2026 Schulich Startup Night alumni finalist, and won both the YSpace Rising Star Award and the Zeifmans Acceler8 Award.

Alongside Doodlepad, I teach part-time at the Schulich School of Business, developing applied courses and workshops across product, innovation, fintech, and AI.
